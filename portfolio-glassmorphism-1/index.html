About Me

From factory floors
to frontends.

I'm a mechanical engineer working in manufacturing and production in the Pune-PCMC region — and I'm deep in the middle of a deliberate transition into software engineering, targeting product companies that care about craft and speed.

Originally from Pandharpur, I built LadeStack solo — a suite of AI-powered dev tools. I don't write code line-by-line; I direct AI tools to ship products fast. Call it vibe coding. I call it leveraged engineering.

My mechanical background gives me something most software folks lack: a discipline around tolerances, systems thinking, and first-principles problem solving.
